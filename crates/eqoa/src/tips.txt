# The loading screens' tips (the GM Jibrish's petition 1778, 2026-11-12: "Add text
# tips to the loading screens. Create one for each command, with a brief one
# sentence description of the command ... Create one hint that loads on a loading
# screen that is randomly displayed"). One tip a line, one sentence each; a line
# starting with # is a note, and a blank line is nothing. Edit freely: `tips.rs`
# reads this file into the client as it is built. GM-only commands stay out.
#
# Each tip starts with its tag (the GM Jibrish's petition 1811): `all:` for the
# client's own features and the game's own commands, shown on every server and
# on the title screen; `heirloom:` for the server's commands, NPCs and features,
# shown only on the Heirloom shard (and on the title screen when Heirloom was
# the server last picked). An untagged tip counts as `heirloom:`.

# The game's own chat commands
all: Did you know you can type /say to speak one line aloud to those near you?
all: Did you know you can type /shout (or /s) to call out to everyone in the zone?
all: Did you know you can type /group (or /g) to speak to your group?
all: Did you know you can type /guild (or /gu) to speak to your guild?
all: Did you know you can type /tell (or /t) and a name to send someone a private message?
all: Did you know you can type /reply (or /r), or press R, to answer the last tell you received?
all: Did you know you can type /follow to follow your target?
all: Did you know you can type /time to see the time in the world?
all: Did you know you can type /alarm and a time, or a number of minutes, to set an alarm?

# Raids, groups and loot
heirloom: Did you know you can type /raid to invite everyone nearby who is not in a group to your raid?
heirloom: Did you know you can type /raidinvite and a name to invite a player, or your target, to your raid?
heirloom: Did you know you can type /raidleave to leave your raid or your group?
heirloom: Did you know a raid's leader or helper can type /raidkick and a name to take a member out of the raid?
heirloom: Did you know you can type /raidleader and a name to hand your raid's lead to another member?
heirloom: Did you know a raid's leader can type /raidhelper and a name to choose a helper who may invite and kick?
heirloom: Did you know a raid's leader can type /raidassist and a name to choose the main assist, and anyone can type /raidassist to assist them?
heirloom: Did you know a raid's leader can type /raidloot to choose free for all, need before greed or master looter?
heirloom: Did you know you can type /raidgroup to list your raid's groups, and its leader can move members between them?
heirloom: Did you know a raid's leader or helper can type /raidswap and two names to trade those members' groups?
heirloom: Did you know you can type /rsay to talk to your whole raid?
heirloom: Did you know you can type /readycheck to ask your group or raid whether everyone is ready?
heirloom: Did you know you can type /ready or /notready to answer a ready check?
heirloom: Did you know you can type /need, /greed or /pass to answer a loot roll?
heirloom: Did you know a master looter can type /assign and an item's number and a name to give that item away?
heirloom: Did you know a master looter can type /abandon to let every item on the corpse go to everyone?

# Features
all: Did you know you can turn the graphics up in Options > Extended > Graphics?
all: Did you know you can turn on Dynamic Lighting, Sun Shadows and Ambient Occlusion in Options > Extended > Graphics > Lighting?
all: Did you know you can draw the game sharper with Render Scale in Options > Extended > Graphics > Video?
all: Did you know you can use PCSX2 texture packs in Options > Extended > Graphics > Textures?
all: Did you know you can press F9 to see the world in the art of the 2002 disc?
all: Did you know you can play several characters at once, each in its own window, with a controller for each?
all: Did you know you can set up macros, PCSX2's way, in the Options window's Macros page?
all: Did you know you can change every key and button in the Options window's Controls page, or press F1 in play?
all: Did you know you can press M to open the map, and N to show or hide the minimap?
all: Did you know you can click the compass to open the map?
all: Did you know you can press Z, or L2, to open the quick chat menus?
all: Did you know you can press H to hide everything over the world for a clean view?
all: Did you know you can press Space to jump?
all: Did you know you can press X, or L3, to run without holding the stick?
all: Did you know you can press F to assist: target whatever your target is fighting?
all: Did you know you can press 1 to 6 to target yourself, your group and your pet?
all: Did you know you can hold R2, or C, to scroll back through the chat?
all: Did you know you can select chat lines with the mouse and copy them?
all: Did you know you can Shift+click an item to link it in chat for others to see?
all: Did you know you can hold a quick bar's ability, or right-click it, to have it cast by itself?
all: Did you know you can left-click one of your buffs to see how long it has left, and right-click it (long-press on touch) to remove it?
all: Did you know you can hold R2 and press L2 to walk your buffs, then press Square to remove one (or hold C, press Z, and use Backspace)?
all: Did you know you can turn the DPS meter on in Options > Extended > UI > DPS Meter?
all: Did you know you can choose the colours of your damage and healing numbers in Options > Extended > UI > Number Colors?
all: Did you know you can make loading screens instant in Options > Extended > Other > General?
all: Did you know you can press F11, or Alt+Enter, to play full screen?
