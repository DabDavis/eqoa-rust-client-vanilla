# The loading screens' tips (the GM Jibrish's petition 1778, 2026-11-12: "Add text
# tips to the loading screens. Create one for each command, with a brief one
# sentence description of the command ... Create one hint that loads on a loading
# screen that is randomly displayed"). One tip a line, one sentence each; a line
# starting with # is a note, and a blank line is nothing. Edit freely: `tips.rs`
# reads this file into the client as it is built. GM-only commands stay out.

# The game's own chat commands
Did you know you can type /say to speak one line aloud to those near you?
Did you know you can type /shout (or /s) to call out to everyone in the zone?
Did you know you can type /group (or /g) to speak to your group?
Did you know you can type /guild (or /gu) to speak to your guild?
Did you know you can type /tell (or /t) and a name to send someone a private message?
Did you know you can type /reply (or /r), or press R, to answer the last tell you received?
Did you know you can type /follow to follow your target?
Did you know you can type /time to see the time in the world?
Did you know you can type /alarm and a time, or a number of minutes, to set an alarm?

# The server's commands for everyone
Did you know you can type /help to list the commands everyone can use?
Did you know you can type /who to see who is in the world?
Did you know you can type /tar (or /target) and a name to target the nearest one by that name?
Did you know you can type /invite and a name to invite a player to your group?
Did you know you can type /stuck to return to your bind point when you cannot move?
Did you know you can type /roll to roll a random number everyone near you can see?
Did you know you can type /lfg on to let others know you are looking for a group?
Did you know you can type /record to see your combat record, or /record and a name to see someone else's?
Did you know you can type /bug and a few words, or press F3, to send a bug report to the GMs?
Did you know you can type /filter to turn the profanity filter on or off?
Did you know you can type /tutorial to read the welcome guide again?
Did you know you can type /duel to challenge your target to a duel?
Did you know you can type /fish to cast a line with the fishing pole in your hand?
Did you know you can type /pvp to turn your player-versus-player flag on or off?
Did you know you can type /home to visit your own home and invite guests to it?
Did you know you can type /ctf to see the capture the flag arena's match and sign up for it?
Did you know you can type /merc to give orders to your mercenary?
Did you know you can type /familiar to see or dismiss your familiar?
Did you know you can type /campfix to report a hunting camp that needs fixing?
Did you know you can type /mark to mark a spot in your open camp report?
Did you know you can type /testinvis to keep creatures from attacking you on sight while you test?

# Raids, groups and loot
Did you know you can type /raid to invite everyone nearby who is not in a group to your raid?
Did you know you can type /raidinvite and a name to invite a player, or your target, to your raid?
Did you know you can type /raidleave to leave your raid or your group?
Did you know a raid's leader or helper can type /raidkick and a name to take a member out of the raid?
Did you know you can type /raidleader and a name to hand your raid's lead to another member?
Did you know a raid's leader can type /raidhelper and a name to choose a helper who may invite and kick?
Did you know a raid's leader can type /raidassist and a name to choose the main assist, and anyone can type /raidassist to assist them?
Did you know a raid's leader can type /raidloot to choose free for all, need before greed or master looter?
Did you know you can type /raidgroup to list your raid's groups, and its leader can move members between them?
Did you know a raid's leader or helper can type /raidswap and two names to trade those members' groups?
Did you know you can type /rsay to talk to your whole raid?
Did you know you can type /readycheck to ask your group or raid whether everyone is ready?
Did you know you can type /ready or /notready to answer a ready check?
Did you know you can type /need, /greed or /pass to answer a loot roll?
Did you know a master looter can type /assign and an item's number and a name to give that item away?
Did you know a master looter can type /abandon to let every item on the corpse go to everyone?

# This client's own commands
Did you know you can type /dps to show the DPS meter, and /dps reset to start it over?
Did you know you can type /autocast to list or set the abilities your quick bars fire by themselves?
Did you know you can type /autotarget to have whatever is attacking you targeted whenever you have no target?
Did you know you can type /hud to drag the pieces of your HUD wherever you like, and /hud reset to put them back?

# Features
Did you know you can turn the graphics up in Options > Extended > Graphics?
Did you know you can turn on Dynamic Lighting, Sun Shadows and Ambient Occlusion in Options > Extended > Graphics > Lighting?
Did you know you can draw the game sharper with Render Scale in Options > Extended > Graphics > Video?
Did you know you can use PCSX2 texture packs in Options > Extended > Graphics > Textures?
Did you know you can press F9 to see the world in the art of the 2002 disc?
Did you know you can play several characters at once, each in its own window, with a controller for each?
Did you know you can set up macros, PCSX2's way, in the Options window's Macros page?
Did you know you can change every key and button in the Options window's Controls page, or press F1 in play?
Did you know you can press M to open the map, and N to show or hide the minimap?
Did you know you can click the compass to open the map?
Did you know you can press Z, or L2, to open the quick chat menus?
Did you know you can press H to hide everything over the world for a clean view?
Did you know you can press Space to jump?
Did you know you can press X, or L3, to run without holding the stick?
Did you know you can press F to assist: target whatever your target is fighting?
Did you know you can press 1 to 6 to target yourself, your group and your pet?
Did you know you can hold R2, or C, to scroll back through the chat?
Did you know you can select chat lines with the mouse and copy them?
Did you know you can Shift+click an item to link it in chat for others to see?
Did you know you can hold a quick bar's ability, or right-click it, to have it cast by itself?
Did you know you can click one of your buffs to see how long it has left, and click it again to remove it?
Did you know you can hold R2 and press L2, or hold C and press Z, to walk your buffs and remove one?
Did you know you can turn the DPS meter on in Options > Extended > UI > DPS Meter?
Did you know you can choose the colours of your damage and healing numbers in Options > Extended > UI > Number Colors?
Did you know you can make loading screens instant in Options > Extended > Other > General?
Did you know you can press F11, or Alt+Enter, to play full screen?
